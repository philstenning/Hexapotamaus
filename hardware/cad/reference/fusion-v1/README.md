# Fusion 360 parts from the 2021 build (reference only)

Exported from the owner's original Fusion 360 designs on 2026-11-20. They are kept for reference: the new leg is modelled from scratch in SolidCraft (`hardware/cad/`), and nothing here is printed again as is.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `servo_motor.step` | The owner's LX-16A model. Origin on the shaft axis at the top face. Its holes are 0.2-0.9 mm off Hiwonder's drawing; see [lx-16a-dimensions.md](../../../../docs/hardware/lx-16a-dimensions.md) |
| `motor_1.dxf`, `motor_2.dxf` | The sketches behind it: the top (shaft) face and the bottom face |
| `bottom-bracket-2021v1.step` | Printed bracket on the servo's bottom face. Its hole positions match Hiwonder's drawing |
| `Arm.step` | U arm with the horn built in (no Hiwonder horn): a Ø19.2 hub 1.4 mm above the case, a Ø6.2 bore 3.5 deep over the spline, an M3 × 6 countersunk screw that clamps the PLA bore onto the spline so it forms its own teeth, 4 × Ø2 holes on a 14 mm circle, and a Ø5.6 socket over the passive boss on the far side, where an M3 × 8 button head and washer, backed off a quarter turn and greased, acts as a plain bearing |
| `joiner.step` | Joiner part (two Ø8 bosses 20 mm apart); its role is to note |
| `bottom-bracket-sketch2.dxf`, `-sketch3.dxf`, `-sketch5.dxf` | The bottom bracket's sketches: the servo cross-section it fits round (24.2 wide, lip to lip 32.8, with the 3 mm top ridge and the bottom panel), its 26.6 × 38 outline, and the end view with the two Ø2 holes at ±10.2 |
